## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined synthesis between traditional ancestral reverence and institutional-grade financial transparency. It evokes solemnity, enduring trust, and quiet prestige, treating family genealogy and collective capital with the precision of an archive and the tactile warmth of heirloom stationery.

The design movement is **Warm Editorial**:
- **Editorial Archival Sensibility:** Inspired by printed pedigrees, heritage publications, and ledger notebooks. Generous proportional white space allows genealogical trees and transaction ledgers to breathe.
- **Warm Tactile Underpinnings:** Moving decisively away from stark, sterile fintech grays and clinical blues, using cream-paper hues, subtle tactile dividers, and organic earth tones.
- **Modern Institutional Rigor:** High-contrast structural data grids, dedicated monospace indexing for lineage identifiers, and immaculate alignment provide indisputable transparency.

## Colors

The palette draws from natural parchment, mineral pigments, and historical seals to project warmth, provenance, and financial clarity.

### Primary & Accent
- **Primary (Terracotta / Coral):** `#cc785c` — The core brand color, used for primary actions, pedigree branch highlights, and focal anchors.
- **Secondary (Emerald Ledger):** `#2e8b43` — Positive ledger balance, recorded revenues, and verified lineage connections.
- **Tertiary (Warm Amber):** `#b36e1b` — Artifacts, physical ancestral assets, pending reconciliations, and historical archives.
- **Critical / Outflow (Brick Red):** `#c64545` — Expenses, critical discrepancies, pending obligations, and overdue dues.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Warm Canvas (Background):** `#faf9f5` — The foundation layer mimicking aged, unbleached acid-free paper.
- **Warm Cream Surface (Containers & Cards):** `#f5f0e8` — Elevated surface for cards, panels, and floating drawers.
- **Warm Cream Band (Section Alt):** `#efe9de` — Used for alternating bands, table headers, and structural grouping.
- **Hairline Border:** `#e6dfd8` — Muted separator ensuring clean structure without mechanical harshness.

### Typography & Content Tone
- **Deep Espresso (Primary Text):** `#141413` — Near-black charcoal with warm undertones for high-contrast legibility.
- **Charcoal Medium (Secondary Text):** `#3d3d3a` — Metadata, secondary labels, and descriptive notes.
- **Muted Stone (Tertiary Text):** `#6c6a64` — Footnotes, genealogical timestamps, and placeholder states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a deliberate contrast between historic authority and data clarity:
- **Display & Headlines (Merriweather):** Carries literary dignity, tradition, and academic gravity. Used for major branch names, patriarch/matriarch records, fund titles, and key annual statements.
- **Interface & Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, open, and exceptionally legible across compact devices. Used for narrative entries, form inputs, dialogs, and balance amounts.
- **Archival Code & Ledger Labels (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced, tabular numbers for currency formatting, generational numbering (`GEN-04-B02`), and ledger entry hashes to reinforce auditability.

## Layout & Spacing

The system uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by comfortable margins reminiscent of wide editorial book layouts.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 40px (`2.5rem`) margins. Content spans standard units (e.g., 3 columns for genealogical meta, 9 columns for transaction manifests).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Dual panes collapse into sequential tabbed cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Lineage trees shift from canvas branching to vertical indented archival lists.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids synthetic drop shadows, relying on **Tonal Layers** and **Hairline Precision**:
- **Layering Principle:** The base canvas is `#faf9f5`. Functional panels and cards rest on `#f5f0e8`. Sub-sections, table headers, and inputs sit on `#efe9de` or `#ffffff`.
- **Hairlines:** Separations and bounds use a razor-sharp `1px solid #e6dfd8` outline.
- **Selective Ambient Lift:** Modals and contextual menus use an extra-soft ambient shadow with subtle warm-charcoal diffusion (`0 10px 30px -5px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.06)`), feeling like paper floating on paper.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances tailored structure with organic approachability:
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs):** Set precisely to `8px` corner radius, creating a sturdy, dependable feel.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Set to `12px` corner radius for natural framing of ledgers and family profiles.
- **Status Pills, Tags, and Genealogic Badges:** Fully circular (`9999px`), invoking lacquer stamp seals and catalog markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#cc785c`, text `#ffffff`, border-radius `8px`, font-weight `600`. Hover state deepens to `#b56348`.
- **Secondary / Ledger Action:** Background `#f5f0e8`, border `1px solid #e6dfd8`, text `#141413`. Hover state shifts to `#efe9de`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#c64545`, text `#ffffff`.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- Background `#f5f0e8` framed with a uniform `1px solid #e6dfd8` border. Corner radius is `12px`.
- Padding uses `space-lg` (`24px`). Internal sub-groupings use the `#efe9de` warm cream band with `8px` inner radius.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#ffffff` against the `#f5f0e8` card surface for distinct input affordance. Border `1px solid #e6dfd8`, radius `8px`.
- Focus state: `1px solid #cc785c` with a subtle `2px` focus halo in `rgba(204, 120, 92, 0.2)`. No cold blue states.

### Status Chips & Lineage Badges
- Shape: `9999px` full pill. Font: `JetBrains Mono` label-ledger style (`11px`, all caps).
- **Revenue / Confirmed:** Background `rgba(46, 139, 67, 0.12)`, text `#2e8b43`, border `1px solid rgba(46, 139, 67, 0.25)`.
- **Expense / Warning:** Background `rgba(198, 69, 69, 0.12)`, text `#c64545`, border `1px solid rgba(198, 69, 69, 0.25)`.
- **Artifact / In-Review:** Background `rgba(179, 110, 27, 0.12)`, text `#b36e1b`, border `1px solid rgba(179, 110, 27, 0.25)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded `4px` (checkbox) and `9999px` (radio). Unchecked: `1px solid #e6dfd8` on white. Checked: `#cc785c` background with crisp white icon.

### Ancestral Lineage Node (Custom Component)
- Avatar or seal badge encased in an `8px` frame with generation marker chip (`code-id`). Connector branches rendered in `1.5px solid #cc785c` with directional flow indicators.
