## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital sanctuary for family lineage, memory preservation, and ancestral ceremonies. It bridges multi-generational divides—serving elderly clan elders who demand absolute clarity and legibility, alongside younger generations seeking an intuitive, engaging connection to their roots.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Warmth** with **Vietnamese Traditional Solemnity**:
- **Trang trọng & Ấm cúng (Solemn & Nurturing):** Visual gravity achieved through deep crimson lacquer tones and golden ceremonial highlights, balanced by organic, tactile warmth rather than cold corporate minimalism.
- **Tối giản & Tinh tế (Minimal & Refined):** Elimination of visual clutter, ornate faux-traditional skeuomorphism, or overwhelming decorative filigree. Respect for lineage is communicated through generous whitespace, deliberate pacing, structured layout rhythm, and impeccable Vietnamese typographic rendering.
- **Universal Accessibility (Hiếu Kính & Hòa Hợp):** Touch ergonomics and typographic scale prioritize aging eyes and differing motor control levels, ensuring dignity and independence for senior clan members.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in ceremonial heritage materials: lacquer, gold leaf, weathered silk, and warm parchment.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#8B1E1E` - Đỏ Huyết Dụ / Maroon Lacquer):** The anchor of solemnity, lineage authority, and respect. Used for primary navigation states, dominant button actions, major genealogical branch headers, and patriarchal/matriarchal milestones.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Vàng Hoàng Kim / Imperial Gold):** Communicates honor, memorial significance, ritual anniversary highlights, and verified lineage badges. Avoid using pure yellow; maintain warm, gilded depth.
- **Neutral Background (`#FDFCF9` - Trắng Ngà / Warm Ivory):** Replaces clinical pure white with an organic, resting paper-like hue that reduces eye fatigue during long reading sessions.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#2A2A2A` - Xám Đen Sâu):** Deep charcoal ink delivering WCAG AAA contrast ratio (exceeding 13:1 against ivory) for primary body copy.
- **Secondary Text (`#5A5A5A` - Xám Tro):** For metadata, historical dates, and branch indicators.
- **Border & Surface Dividers (`#E5E5E5` - Xám Nhạt Ấm):** Subtle boundary definitions that separate generations and records without breaking spatial continuity.

### Functional & Semantic Roles
- **Success (`#1B873F` - Xanh Lá Tự Nhiên):** Lineage validation, completed rituals, verified biographical records.
- **Warning (`#E67E22` - Cam Ấm):** Pending approvals, 24-hour ceremonial reminders, conflict in dates.
- **Danger (`#D32F2F` - Đỏ Tươi Nổi Bật):** Overdue notices, rejected records, deletion cautions.

## Typography

Typographic scale and hierarchy are engineered for maximum diacritical clarity in the Vietnamese writing system. **Be Vietnam Pro** is selected as the unified typeface across all roles for its native support of stacked diacritics (dấu thanh, dấu mũ, dấu móc) without clipping or uneven vertical rhythm.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Strict Size Floor:** Absolute body text size never falls below `14px` (`0.875rem`), with preferred reading default at `16px` (`1rem`) to support senior eyesight.
- **Line Heights:** Set generously (`1.6` to `1.7x`) to accommodate complex diacritics and promote unhurried, comfortable reading.
- **Numeral Treatment:** Genealogical dates, lunar/solar calendar pairings, and generational branches must use tabular or carefully spaced numerals to prevent visual wobble in family tree nodes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a hybrid structured grid that scales from expansive family tree views down to vertical commemorative timelines on handheld devices.

### Breakpoints & Canvas
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 2.5rem canvas margins and 1.5rem gutters. Used for wide pedigree trees, generational comparison views, and detailed archival registers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 2rem canvas margins and 1.25rem gutters. Sidebar interactions collapse into contextual drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem canvas margins and 1rem gutters. Horizontal tree visualizers transition smoothly into vertical ancestor cascades and card stacks.

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Anti-Density Philosophy:** Generous padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) isolates discrete family branches and ritual sequences, preventing cognitive overload.
- **Touch Target Safeguard:** All interactive touch points are allocated minimum structural bounds of `48px x 48px`, surrounded by at least `space-sm` separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with dignified restraint, utilizing delicate physical layers reminiscent of fine stationery rather than dramatic synthetic drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Default flat tone `#FDFCF9`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Member Plates):** `#FFFFFF` surface resting on canvas with a crisp border (`1px solid #E5E5E5`) and an ambient, warm shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Nodes & Hover States):** Lifted slightly with enhanced warmth: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(139, 30, 30, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.04)`. Border shifts to subtle accent tint.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Memorial Dialogs & Drawers):** Elevated above the surface with dimming overlay (`rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(2px)`): `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 8px rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular uses balanced curves to evoke warmth, approachability, and unity, avoiding both cold industrial corners and overly playful pill contours.

- **Base Radii (`roundedness: 2`):** Primary interactive elements like inputs, dropdowns, and button containers use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards & Generational Nodes (`rounded-lg`):** Sized at `1rem` (16px) to frame portrait photographs, biography summaries, and ritual itineraries in soft, respectful boundaries.
- **Modal Sheets & Overlays (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) on top edges for mobile action sheets and complete container bounds on desktop viewports.
- **Avatars & Crests:** Exact circles (`rounded-full`) reserved for ancestor portraits and generational lineage tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Touch Targets
- **Dimensions:** Minimum height of `48px` (`h-12`) and minimum width of `48px` across all screen sizes. Mobile touch zones never drop below this threshold.
- **Primary Button:** `#8B1E1E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-lg` styling. Subtle hover state darkens to `#731818`. Focus ring uses `#D4AF37` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined style with `1.5px solid #8B1E1E`, `#8B1E1E` text, and transparent surface that acquires an ivory-warm tint (`rgba(139, 30, 30, 0.04)`) upon interaction.
- **Honorary / Accent Button:** Reserved for memorial donations, lineage verification, or ritual bookings: `#D4AF37` background with `#2A2A2A` deep charcoal text for high contrast.

### Cards (Generational & Event Records)
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by `1px solid #E5E5E5`, `rounded-lg` (16px) corners, and Level 1 ambient shadow.
- **Padding:** `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) internal spacing on desktop, `1rem` (`space-md`) on mobile screens.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Clear division between generation badge, ceremonial dates (Lunar & Solar pairings), branch title, and biographical links.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Fixed height of `48px`, border `1px solid #E5E5E5`, background `#FFFFFF`, font `body-md`. Focus transitions to border `#8B1E1E` with a 1px ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sized at `24x24px` with a minimum surrounding touch wrapper of `48x48px`. Active state filled with `#8B1E1E` checkmark in white.

### Chips & Generational Tags
- **Generation Chips:** Pill-shaped or soft-rounded (`rounded-md`), `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`, displaying "Đời thứ X" (Generation X). Uses subdued crimson tint (`rgba(139, 30, 30, 0.08)`) with `#8B1E1E` text.
- **Memorial / Status Badges:** Semantic pairing with high-contrast text:
  - Validated/Active: `#1B873F` background (10% tint), dark green text.
  - Pending Clan Approval: `#E67E22` background (10% tint), dark amber text.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Pedigree Node Card:** Displays ancestor portrait, title (Cụ Tổ, Trưởng Tộc, v.v.), birth/death dates, and expansion indicator for descendants.
- **Giỗ Chạp & Event Timeline Item:** Dual calendar display showing Âm lịch (Lunar) prominently alongside Dương lịch (Solar), with calendar sync actions and attendant RSVPs.
